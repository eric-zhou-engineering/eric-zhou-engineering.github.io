Engineering Deep Dive

Project: Thermal Reliability System

A comprehensive look at the R&D lifecycle: from complex high-voltage PCB layout to rapid breadboard prototyping, final assembly, and mass production scaling.

01

Design & Layout

The challenge was to design a high-power driver capable of handling 135V at 15A. Using Altium Designer, I engineered a multi-layer PCB focusing on:

  • Creepage and clearance optimization for high-voltage safety.
  • Thermal relief strategies for high-current paths.
  • High-side MOSFET driving circuit implementation.
02

Prototyping & Validation

Theoretical designs must be validated. I moved rapidly from breadboard Proof-of-Concepts (POC) to the first fabricated modules. Bench testing confirmed the 1MHz switching frequency capability and thermal stability under load.

"Transitioning from breadboard wires to a structured test jig reduced setup time by 90% during the validation phase."

Initial POC
03

Mass Production

Once validated, the design was scaled. I managed the panelization and component sourcing for mass production. The final delivery included multiple batches of consistent, high-quality driver boards that integrated seamlessly into the customer's inspection tools.
